02 / Evidence, not assertion

Open the documents. Judge the work.

Click a role to inspect its captured job posting, submitted résumé, and cover letter when available. The Certn dossier also includes the archived combined application packet. Original PDF documents are served as archived; MHTML and some word-processor files are presented in simplified reading formats.

Application confirmations and recruiter correspondence are not published. Some newly archived PDFs are linked through their application folder rather than an individual file URL. A document marked Not archived really is absent from the provided application material.
