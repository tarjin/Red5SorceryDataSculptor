BM-06MATERIAL NON-BLOCKING

The golden fixtures encode impossible timelines and an unreachable Suitcase

Fixtures: all seven success/*.txt; Inventory/__DS__INV__20261007T120000000Z__…001.txt · BM: W002-RCP-006W002-RCP-ACC-001W002-STOR-018.3W002-STOR-022W002-INV-003

Evidence

  • E In every success Receipt, the published artifact's UTC falls outside its own [RUN_STARTED_UTC, RUN_FINISHED_UTC]. In SUCCESS__MATERIALIZE, the RID, COL and MAP are stamped 14:59:59.000, eleven seconds before the run started at 15:00:10. In the other six, the artifact is stamped after RECEIPT_UTC; for example, INVENTORY publishes its INV at 15:01:00 inside a Receipt written at 15:00:22, and CATALOG_STORES publishes its CAT at 15:06:00 inside a Receipt written at 15:01:12.
  • E BM never defines RUN_STARTED_UTC or RUN_FINISHED_UTC. They appear only as names in the W002-RCP-006 envelope list. P2-03 and S-04 require transaction UTCs to be truthful host observations made during publication.
  • E The INV fixture lists the run WKO …000000000002.txt, alpha.csv and beta.csv, but no WKO MAP and no CER. W002-STOR-018.3 publishes the WKO and its WKO MAP in one save transaction, an unregistered WKO cannot Run, and W002-STOR-022 locks analytical execution until a CER exists in the root. W002-INV-003 inventories every regular root file.

Why it matters

  • I A conforming implementation with a monotonic clock seam cannot reproduce these bytes. Matching them requires a seam that runs time backwards, which teaches the opposite of P2-03. A product whose creed is "I know what it actually did" should not keep, as its reference evidence, Receipts that report artifacts created after the Receipt.

Direction. Define RUN_STARTED_UTC and RUN_FINISHED_UTC, including their ordering relative to transaction UTCs and RECEIPT_UTC. Regenerate the fixture timestamps so that WKO ≤ run start ≤ transaction UTC ≤ run finish ≤ RECEIPT_UTC. Make the INV fixture list a reachable Suitcase, or state that the INV fixture is a format oracle built by a harness.
